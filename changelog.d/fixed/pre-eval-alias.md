- **[engine]** An `alias` or `alias_method` inside a `pre_eval:` monkey-patch now publishes the new name project-wide as a `def` there does, so a call such as `3.to_m` after `alias to_m to_modint` no longer reports `call.undefined-method`. Within one `pre_eval:` file, a name defined twice no longer reports `pre-eval.duplicate-declaration`, and the later definition wins, as at runtime. ([#1702](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1702), [#1705](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1705))
