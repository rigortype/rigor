- **[rigor-rbs-inline]** A method declared both in `sig/` and by an inline `# @rbs` / `#:` annotation is now compared instead of the `sig/` side always winning: when one declaration refines the other, the more precise one binds without a diagnostic (`Symbol` in `sig/` and `:asc | :desc` inline read as `:asc | :desc`, `String` and `non-empty-string` as `non-empty-string`), and when the two contradict, the new `rbs.contradicting-signature` error is reported at the `sig/` line and the `sig/` side binds. The same error covers a `rigor:v1:return:` / `rigor:v1:param:` refinement disjoint from its own declared type. Only a pair Rigor cannot rank still drops the inline side with a `source-rbs-annotation-not-honoured` info. The error fires only on a proven contradiction: overloads are paired by what they declare rather than their order, and a module, two classes of your own, or a keyword against a positional `Hash` stay informational. **Upgrade note:** a contradicting pair that used to be an info now fails `rigor check`, so regenerate the stale signature or fix the annotation; and since a more precise inline parameter type now binds where `sig/` used to win, a call the old `sig/` type accepted can become a new call-site error.
