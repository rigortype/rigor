- **[engine]** Overload selection no longer counts a splat argument as one positional, so an overload the splat's count may reach is no longer ruled out by arity alone: with `(a: Integer) -> Integer | (Hash[Symbol, String]) -> String`, `ph(*xs, a: "s")` is `String` instead of `Integer`, and `ph(*xs, a: "s").upcase` no longer reports `call.undefined-method`. Where several overloads may apply, their returns join, and the block parameters bind only where those overloads agree. ([#1801](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1801), [#1810](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1810))
