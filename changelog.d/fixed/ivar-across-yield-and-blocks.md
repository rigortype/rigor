- **[engine]** An instance variable no longer keeps its pre-call value past a `yield`, a `super` or a call of a local proc (`blk.call`, `blk.()`) — as a statement, in a condition, in another call's receiver or arguments, or inside a block — nor past a block on another receiver whose body calls a method on `self`, so a condition on it afterwards no longer reports `flow.always-truthy-condition` on correct code.
