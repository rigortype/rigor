- **[engine]** A constant referenced inside a compact namespace declaration — `Rails` written in `module MyApp::Inner`, `User` written in `class Admin::UsersController` — now resolves the way Ruby resolves it, to the top-level constant, instead of to a same-named one under the enclosing namespace; the wrong constant was also reported as `undefined method` on correct code, and the fix covers plain constant reads, `is_a?`/`kind_of?`/`instance_of?`, `case`/`when` and `Class === x` alike. The nested spelling of the same class keeps reaching the nearer constant, exactly as before. ([#652](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/652))
