- **[engine]** A call whose argument mixes an untyped value with a precise one now reaches every overload the untyped part can take, so `0 + (flag ? untyped : 1)` reads `Dynamic[… | Float | Integer | …]` instead of `Integer`, and `rigor sig-gen` no longer proposes such a narrow return. ([#1675](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1675), [#1677](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1677))
