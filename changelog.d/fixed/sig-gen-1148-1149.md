- **[sig-gen]** An empty hash fallback (`return {} if ...`, `table[key] || {}`) no longer leaves a stray `{}` arm beside a `Hash[K, V]` arm, so `Hash[String, Integer] | {}` is proposed as `Hash[String, Integer]`. ([#2311](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/2311))
  - A lone `{}`, `{}` beside a non-`Hash` arm, and an open or non-empty hash shape are proposed as before.
