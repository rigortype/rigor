- **[engine]** An implicit-self or `self.` `gets` / `readline` condition now narrows `$_` as `$stdin.gets` does, in the script body and in methods — `while gets; $_; end` reads `String` in the loop and `nil` after it — unless the file shows a `self` whose reader may be written in Ruby: a mixin into `main` or `Object`, a reader defined on `main`, a block whose `self` is rebound (`instance_eval`, `class_exec`, `define_method`, …), or a class whose ancestry may hold a Ruby reader (`DelegateClass(File)`, `Tempfile`, `CSV`, an unresolved superclass or mixin).
