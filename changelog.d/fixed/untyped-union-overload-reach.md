- **[engine]** A call whose argument mixes an untyped value with a precise one, such as `flag ? untyped : 1` or an element read from `Array[untyped] | Array[Integer]`, now reaches every overload the untyped part can take instead of the first one the precise part allows. `0 + w` reads `Dynamic[… | Float | Integer | …]` rather than `Integer`, `Array(x)` and `/re/ =~ s` no longer pin `[]` and `nil`, `([true] * n).first` no longer reports `call.undefined-method` for `String`, and `rigor sig-gen` stops proposing such narrow returns. ([#1675](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1675), [#1677](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1677))
