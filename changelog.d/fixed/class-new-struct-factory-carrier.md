- **[engine]** Struct and Data factory carriers (`Type::StructClass`, `Type::DataClass`) are now accepted as `Class` subtypes during parameter matching, eliminating false positive `call.argument-type-mismatch` reports when passing `Struct.new(...)` or `Data.define(...)` to `Class.new`. Classes constructed via `Class.new(Struct.new(...))` and `Class.new(Data.define(...))` also preserve member readers for in-body dispatch and downstream folding. ([#634](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/634), [#687](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/687))
