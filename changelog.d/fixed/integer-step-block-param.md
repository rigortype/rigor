- **[engine]** `1.step(n, 2) { |i| i.even? }` no longer reports `undefined method 'even?' for Numeric` on correct code. `Integer#step`'s block parameter binds `Integer` when the limit and the step are Integer, and `Dynamic[Numeric]` when one is untyped, since `1.step(n, 0.5)` yields Floats; a Float operand or receiver still reports.
