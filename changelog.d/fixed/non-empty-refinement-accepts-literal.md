- **[check]** A `non-empty-array[T]`, `non-empty-hash[K, V]` or `non-zero-int` in an `RBS::Extended` `return:` or `param:` override now accepts a value that is never empty or zero — an array literal without splats such as `[1, 2, 3]`, a hash literal of literal keys such as `{ name: 1 }`, or an `Integer` range that skips `0` such as `xs.size + 1` — instead of reporting `def.return-type-mismatch` or `call.argument-type-mismatch` on it; `[]`, `{}` and a range covering `0` still fire.
