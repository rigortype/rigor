- **[engine]** A `using` of a module Rigor cannot read (a gem's refinement module, one required from outside the analysed paths, or one whose `refine` is computed or reached through `send(:refine, …)`) no longer has its calls checked or typed against the methods the refinement may replace, and a refine body that defines a method through `alias`, `alias_method`, `define_method`, `attr_*` or `import_methods` now silences the checks on that method under `using`, so `call.wrong-arity` and `call.undefined-method` no longer fire on such correct code. ([#1811](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1811))
