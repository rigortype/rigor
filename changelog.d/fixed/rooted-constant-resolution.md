- **[engine]** A constant written with a leading `::` now resolves to the top-level constant instead of a same-named one in the surrounding namespace, so `::Rails.logger` inside a `module MyApp` that also defines `MyApp::Rails` types as the framework's `Rails` and no longer draws spurious method errors. ([#614](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/614), [#642](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/642))
