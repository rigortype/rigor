- **[engine]** A project signature that declares one member of the vendored `prime` signatures, such as an `Integer#prime?` shim, now stands down only that member (or, for a class/module or generic-arity clash, only that type) instead of the whole vendored copy, so `12.prime_division` and `Prime.each` keep resolving after `require "prime"`; what stood down is reported as the `:info` notice `rbs.coverage.vendored-signature-stood-down`.
