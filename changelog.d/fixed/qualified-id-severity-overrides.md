- **[config]** `severity_overrides:` now names a plugin's diagnostic by its qualified id (`plugin.<id>.<rule>`, or `plugin.<id>` for the whole plugin) instead of its bare rule, so `load-error: off` no longer silences every plugin's own `load-error`; the plugin loader's rows answer to `plugin_loader.load-error` and the bare `load-error`, and `rigor check` warns about a key that reaches no row. ([#2000](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/2000))
