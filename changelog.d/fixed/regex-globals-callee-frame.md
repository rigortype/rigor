- **[engine]** A call into a method defined in Ruby, such as `log("parsed")` or `warn "debug"` between a regex match and a `$1` read, no longer drops the `$~` / `$1` narrowing, because such a method matches in its own frame and cannot rebind its caller's globals; `eval`, a `send` with a computed name, an argument that runs a match, and any implicit-self call in a method that also holds a block that may match still drop it. ([#1374](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1374))
