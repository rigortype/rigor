- **[rigor check]** A visibility that may not hold — a `def` after a `private`, `protected`, `public` or `module_function` call written under a condition, inside a block or in a `begin`, or a `private :x` under a condition — is now read as uncertain, so the override and private-call visibility lints no longer report against it: a module's `def` after a conditional `module_function` is no longer called a public method an includer's private override narrows. ([#1562](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1562))
