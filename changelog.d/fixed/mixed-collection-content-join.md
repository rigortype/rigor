- **[inference]** A local seeded with a mixed `Array | Hash` union and content-mutated in a block or `while` body now keeps what the body did to its Array member — a `map!` or a `<<` there is no longer dropped in favour of the seed's literal, so `x.first.upcase` after a `map!(&:to_s)` and `y.size == 1` after a `y << 2` stop drawing false `call.undefined-method` / `flow.always-truthy-condition` diagnostics.
