- **[engine]** A `=~` guard no longer refines a Symbol or untyped subject to a String, whichever side the pattern is on (`name =~ /\A\d+\z/` and `/\A\d+\z/ =~ name`), so `name.to_proc` past it is not reported; when the subject may be `nil` or is untyped, the no-match edge reads `$1` as `String?` instead of `nil`, since `NilClass#=~` leaves the previous match bound; and a receiver whose class gets a `=~` from the project (a subclass override, a mixin, or a reopened `String`) now reads `$1` as `String?` on both edges, since that method's match binds `$~` in its own frame. ([#1949](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1949))
