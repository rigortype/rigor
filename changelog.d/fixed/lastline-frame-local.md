- **[engine]** `$_` is now frame-local: a top-level write no longer leaks into method bodies, and a `while gets` / `if gets` condition narrows `$_` to `String` on its truthy edge and `nil` on its falsey edge. The body of a `while` / `until` loop now reads its condition's narrowing also when the body reassigns no local, so `while (line = gets); line.chomp; end` and `skipped.pop.invoke_after(env) while skipped&.first` no longer report a false `call.possible-nil-receiver`. ([#1405](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1405))
