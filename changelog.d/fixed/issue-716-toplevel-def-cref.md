- **[engine]** A method defined at the top level now resolves the constants in its body at the top level, the way Ruby does, instead of under the namespace it happens to be called from — so a `def helper = Post.new` written outside any `class` or `module` names `::Post` even when it is called from inside a `module Admin` that declares its own `Post`, and no longer reports a missing method on the class it never named. ([#716](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/716), [#764](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/764))
