- **[rigor sig-gen]** `rigor sig-gen` now keeps the `nil` a lookup that can miss returns, proposing `def sup: (Hash[Symbol, String] h, Symbol k) -> (String | nil)` for `def sup(h, k) = h[k]` instead of a nil-free `-> String` ([#2249](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/2249)).
  - It covers `Hash#[]`, `Array#[]`, `#first` and `#last`, read directly or through a local, a branch, `&.`, `&&` or `||=`, and in an attribute whose ivar is assigned one; `fetch`, `h[k] || default` and a guard that tests the value stay nil-free, and `rigor check` reads lookups as before.
