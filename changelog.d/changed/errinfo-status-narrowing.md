- **[engine]** `$!` and `$@` now read as the rescued exception and its backtrace inside a `rescue` clause or a rescue modifier's fallback (`rescue A, B` gives `A | B`, a bare `rescue` gives `StandardError`) and return to their earlier binding once the `begin` exits, and `$?` reads as `Process::Status` after a backtick command, `system` or a blocking `Process.wait`, unless the file also runs a `WNOHANG` wait or `waitall`, which can reset it to `nil`.
