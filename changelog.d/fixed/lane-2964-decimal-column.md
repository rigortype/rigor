- **[plugin:activerecord]** A `decimal` column's reader is now typed `Integer` at scale 0 and `BigDecimal` at a non-zero literal scale instead of `Float`, so `Integer` and `BigDecimal` methods declared in RBS no longer report `call.undefined-method`, thank you @takayamaki! ([#2967](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/2967))
  - The `db/schema.rb` DSL reads `t.decimal` and `t.column ..., :decimal` by their literal `scale:`, and `db/structure.sql` reads `numeric(p, s)` and `decimal(p, s)` by `s`.
  - A decimal with no scale, or a non-literal one, stays untyped, because the database adapter decides its class.
