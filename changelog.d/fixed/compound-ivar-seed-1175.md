- **[inference]** Compound ivar writes (`@x ||= v`, `@x &&= v`, `@x op= v`) now contribute to the class-ivar seed the pre-pass builds, so a `||=` that only ever writes a truthy value no longer leaves the ivar pinned to an earlier `Constant[false]` — the shape that false-fired `flow.always-truthy-condition` on `unless @flag` (ADR-58 § WD5's deferred `||=` seeding, reopened by Rigor's own `unit_scan.rb`). `||=` seeds the rvalue union `nil` (the pre-write state a memo idiom's reader can observe), `&&=` seeds the rvalue, and `op=` seeds the widened operator-dispatch result rather than a folded literal.
