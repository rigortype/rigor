- **[rigor check]** A body typed `FalseClass`, `TrueClass` or `NilClass` (as `Array.new(n, false)` is) now satisfies a declared `bool`, `true`, `false` or `nil`, alone or nested (`Array[bool]`, `[nil]`, `Integer?`), so `def.return-type-mismatch` and `call.argument-type-mismatch` no longer fire on such correct code, and overload selection reaches the matching literal arm.
