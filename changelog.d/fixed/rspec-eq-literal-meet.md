- **[plugins]** `rigor-rspec` no longer replaces a local's type after `expect(x).to eq(literal)` or `eql(literal)`, since RSpec runs `x == literal` with `x`'s own `==`: a class with its own `==` (a `ModInt` comparing `to_i` passes `eq(9)`) no longer leaves `x` as the literal and reports `call.undefined-method` on correct code. `x` narrows only where its type is of core classes with built-in equality (`x = rand(10); expect(x).to eq(9)` still gives `9`), and a numeric member of another class is kept under `eq` (`Integer | Float` with `eq(1)` gives `1 | Float`). ([#1807](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1807), [#1917](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1917))
