- **[check]** `disable:` and `# rigor:disable` now reach a plugin's diagnostics by their qualified ID, `plugin.<id>.<rule>` or `plugin.<id>` for the whole plugin. The `# rigor:disable plugin.shoulda-matchers.unknown-column` spelling that the shoulda-matchers manual already documented used to do nothing and now works. `rigor check` warns about a `disable:` entry that cannot reach anything: a plugin's bare rule, a plugin missing from `plugins:`, or a run-level row that only `severity_overrides:` controls. Because `rigor doctor` reports every config warning as a failure, it now fails on these inert entries too. ([#1983](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1983))
