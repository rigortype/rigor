- `call.deprecated-ruby2-keywords` reports a call to the `ruby2_keywords` family Ruby 4.1 deprecates (`Module#ruby2_keywords`, top-level `ruby2_keywords` and `Proc#ruby2_keywords`, removed in 4.4; `Hash.ruby2_keywords_hash?` and `Hash.ruby2_keywords_hash`, removed in 4.5) when `.rigor.yml` sets `target_ruby` explicitly to `"4.1"` or later; the default `target_ruby` and `"latest"` report nothing. ([#1692](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1692), [#1780](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1780))
