- **[inference]** A method called with a double-splatted hash (`target(**h)`) or reached through `...` forwarding (`def forward(...) = target(...)`) now binds its parameters from those call sites instead of analysing its body with `Dynamic[top]` parameters: `target(**h)` binds exactly what the literal `target(a: 1, b: 2)` binds, `**rest` collects the keyword pairs no named parameter consumed, and a shapeless `Hash[Symbol, V]` or opaque value keeps the previous answer. ([#1125](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1125), [#1166](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1166))
