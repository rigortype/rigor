- **[inference]** Destructuring a union right-hand side (`e, *f = cond ? [1, "s"] : [1.0]`, `k, v = hash.find { ... }`) now binds each slot to the join of its members instead of `Dynamic[top]`, keeping a member's `nil` out of a slot another member fills while leaving that slot's `nil` branch reachable, and a value without `to_ary` (`a, b = 1`, `c, d = { k: 1 }`, `e, f = nil`) binds as Ruby's `[value]`; `Delegator`-style wrappers and classes that may convert stay `Dynamic[top]`; a `v == nil` / `v != nil` guard (either side, any equality spelling) over such a slot, or over a lookup like `MAP[key]`, no longer reports `flow.always-truthy-condition`, matching `v.nil?`.
