- **[engine]** `$_` is now frame-local: a top-level write no longer leaks into method bodies, and a condition on a reader Rigor can prove is Ruby's own (`while $stdin.gets`, `if STDIN.gets`, or `gets` on `Kernel`, `ARGF` or an `IO`) narrows `$_` to `String` on its truthy edge and `nil` on its falsey edge; an implicit-self `gets` leaves `$_` untyped, since `self` may define its own. The body of a `while` / `until` loop now reads its condition's narrowing also when the body reassigns no local, so `while (line = STDIN.gets); line.chomp; end` and `skipped.pop.invoke_after(env) while skipped&.first` no longer report a false `call.possible-nil-receiver`. ([#1405](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1405))
