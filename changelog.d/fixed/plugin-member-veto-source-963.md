- **[inference]** A plugin-supplied member of the enclosing `self` — a rigor-activerecord column reader, association or scope, a `dynamic_return` answer, an ADR-16 synthesised method — now stops a same-named top-level `def` binding ahead of it, so `name.upcase` inside a model's own method no longer reads a script's `def name` and reports `undefined method 'upcase' for nil` on working code.
