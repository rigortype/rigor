- **[sig-gen]** `rigor sig-gen --params=observed` now types `Data.define` and `Struct.new` members from the construction sites it observes, so `P.new(name: "a")` gives `def name: () -> String`. ([#2195](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/2195))
  - `.new` and `.[]` count, with keywords or positionals, as do `Data#with` and Struct writer calls. Literals widen to their class.
  - Members are typed only for a class proven plain in every file sig-gen reads: its constant is used only to define, reopen, construct or class-check it, and its body holds only ordinary methods, attributes and constants. Any other use keeps every member `untyped`.
  - A member also stays `untyped` when a seen site may set it through a mapping sig-gen does not read, such as a splat, an index or `send` write, or a writer on a receiver sig-gen cannot type.
