- **[engine]** `rigor unused` no longer lists a constant as unused when a literal `const_get` on `self` reaches it through an `include` / `prepend` / `extend` on a receiver it can name (`K.include(Mix)` in an initializer, or its `send` spellings), `K = Class.new(Base)`, or a method an eval block defines; where the receiver cannot be named, the included module's own literal lookups are kept.
