- **[inference]** `x.nil?`, `!x` and the `x == nil` family over an optimistically nil-free read (`hash[key]`, `array.first`) no longer fold to a `true`/`false` constant — they answer `bool`, so a helper ending in `!h[k].nil?` no longer publishes a constant return that makes the caller's live guard report `flow.always-truthy-condition`.
