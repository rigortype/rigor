- **[inference]** A comparison of a Float against a numeric literal now narrows the branch it holds in: `x > 0.0` types `x` as `Float[0.0..]` inside the `if`, `x < 1.0` as `Float[...1.0]`, `x.between?(0.0, 1.0)` as `Float[0.0..1.0]`, `x.finite?` as `finite-float`, and the `else` of `x.nan?` as `non-nan-float`; the other branch keeps `Float`, because a comparison that fails may have failed on `NaN` ([ADR-109](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/blob/master/docs/adr/109-ruby-native-range-notation.md), [#846](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/846)).
