- **[plugins]** rigor-actionpack now compiles every `app/views/**/*.erb` into a template unit — Erubi when it resolves in your project's bundle, stdlib `ERB` otherwise — so a view's effects appear in `rigor effects` as `view:users/show.html`, a partial that writes to the database reports `io.db.write` at its own line, and `effects.envelopes:` can hold views to a budget; type checks inside templates stay off until you set `view_type_checks: true`.
