- **[engine]** `1.step(n, 2).map { |i| i.even? }` no longer reports `undefined method 'even?' for Numeric` on correct code. Block-less `Integer#step` returns `Enumerator::ArithmeticSequence[Integer]` when the limit and the step are Integer, and `[Dynamic[Numeric]]` when one is untyped, and the sequence's `each`, `map`, `select`, `to_a`, `first` and the rest of its Enumerable surface read that element; a Float operand (`Float::INFINITY` included, whose sequence yields Floats) or receiver still reports. ([#1794](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/issues/1794), [#1809](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/1809))
