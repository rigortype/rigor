- **[check]** `call.unresolved-toplevel` no longer fires inside a `Const = Struct.new(…) do … end` body, nor in its `Data.define` / `Class.new` / `Module.new` twins: the block is the class's own body, so its member reads, `attr_*` macros and `def`s are now scoped to the constant's class the way the bare and `class X < Struct.new(…)` spellings already were — and a struct member that shadows a `Kernel` method (`lambda`) now reads as the member instead of as a `Proc`.
