- **[types]** Float ranges: a `%a{rigor:v1:…}` payload may now bound a Float with the Ruby range literal that covers it, `Float[0.0..1.0]`, `Float[0.0...1.0]`, `Float[0.0..]` or `Float[0.0...Float::INFINITY]`, and two names cover the ranges people mean most, `non-nan-float` (every Float except NaN) and `finite-float` (every Float except NaN and the infinities); no bounded range contains NaN, a bounded Float dispatches as a Float, and comparison narrowing to these ranges is still to come ([ADR-109](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/blob/master/docs/adr/109-ruby-native-range-notation.md), [#844](https://github.com/rigortype/rigor/pull/844)).
